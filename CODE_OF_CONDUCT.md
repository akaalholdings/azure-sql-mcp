# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and professional community.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment:

- Being respectful in communication and review feedback
- Focusing on technical outcomes rather than personal criticism
- Accepting constructive feedback and correcting mistakes quickly

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory language
- Personal attacks, trolling, or insulting remarks
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.
Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions
that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it through a private GitHub
security advisory or direct private communication with maintainers.

All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
